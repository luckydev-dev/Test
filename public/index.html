Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Snapp is intentionally focused on public media and a calm, straightforward experience.

What links can I download?+

Public Instagram posts, Reels, videos, and carousels. Private posts, stories, live videos, and links that require a login are not supported.

Do I need an Instagram account?+

No. Snapp never asks for your Instagram credentials and does not require an account to download public media.

Where is my download saved?+

Your browser handles the download just like any other file. Snapp does not keep a copy of the media or your link history on a server.

Can I download someone else's content?+

Only download content you have permission to use. Respect the original creator's copyright and Instagram's terms when saving or sharing media.
